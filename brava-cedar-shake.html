Why Brava

Cedar Character. Composite Performance.

Real cedar looks beautiful for a few seasons — then comes the splitting, curling, moss, and endless maintenance. Brava gives you everything you love about cedar, permanently.

01

Indistinguishable from Cedar

Brava's Colorcast™ mineral-infusion process runs colour through every inch of the tile — not just the surface. Multi-tonal variation in every batch mirrors nature itself.

02

50-Year Transferable Manufacturer Warranty

One of the strongest warranties in roofing — and fully transferable to the next owner. A genuine, documented selling feature when you list your home.

03

Zero Maintenance

No splitting, curling, rot, or moss treatments. No replacing individual shakes every windstorm. Install it once and forget about your roof for decades.

04

Built for BC's Climate

Tested against high wind, freeze-thaw cycling, and coastal moisture — precisely the conditions your Lower Mainland or Fraser Valley roof endures through 1,200mm of annual rainfall.

05

Environmentally Responsible

Manufactured from recycled materials and fully recyclable at end of life. The sustainable choice for homes that want cedar looks without harvesting old growth.

06

Deeper, Richer Profile

Brava shake is up to 2× thicker than competing composites, with tile thickness from ⅝" to ⅞" creating the dramatic shadow lines that make cedar roofs so striking.

Our Process

Simple. Transparent. No Surprises.

One team, one fixed price, and full documentation from first inspection to warranty registration.

01

Free Inspection

A experienced roofing inspector assesses your existing roof, measures precisely, and walks you through Brava colour and profile options for your home's architecture.

02

Detailed Scope & Quote

You receive a fixed-price, itemised written quote. You'll know every material, every labour cost, and every warranty before signing anything.

03

Professional Installation

Our Brava factory-trained crew installs to exact manufacturer specification — protecting both product performance and the 50-year warranty.

04

Warranty & Follow-Up

We register your 50-year Brava warranty with the manufacturer, deliver all documentation in writing, and follow up with a 12-month check-in.
