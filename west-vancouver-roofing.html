Common Questions

Roofing in West Vancouver
Answered.

What does a roof replacement cost in West Vancouver?
Roof replacement in West Vancouver typically ranges from $18,000 to $45,000+ for luxury and estate properties, depending on size, pitch complexity, material selection, and existing deck condition. Stonnex provides a written fixed-price quote after a complimentary on-site inspection.
Do you install metal roofing in West Vancouver?
Yes. Standing seam metal and metal tile systems are available for West Vancouver properties seeking longevity and premium aesthetic. Metal roofing carries a 40-50 year expected lifespan and is well-suited to BC's climate.
How does Stonnex handle complex architectural rooflines?
We are a GAF Certified Contractor and have experience with multi-pitch, high-slope, and custom architectural rooflines common in West Vancouver's estate properties. We assess all penetrations and flashing details before quoting.
Is Stonnex insured to work on high-value properties in West Vancouver?
Yes. Stonnex carries $5M general liability insurance and is WorkSafeBC registered, which meets most strata and high-value property requirements for contractor coverage.
