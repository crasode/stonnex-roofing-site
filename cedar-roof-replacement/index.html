Replacing a Cedar Shake Roof:
Your Three Real Options

Cedar shake roofs were the standard across the Lower Mainland for decades, and thousands are now past 25 years, mossed over, split and leaking. Replacing one is different from a shingle re-roof: cedar sits on skip sheathing with gaps between boards, so whatever goes on next needs a new solid deck first. Stonnex has converted cedar roofs across Vancouver, Abbotsford, Mission and the North Shore, and we are a Brava Preferred Contractor for homeowners who want the cedar look without the cedar.

This page lays out the three honest paths: architectural shingles, Brava composite shake, or new cedar, with the cost, lifespan and fire-rating implications of each.

  • Full removal of shakes and skip sheathing; new plywood deck included in every cedar conversion
  • Option 1: architectural shingles, the most cost-effective path
  • Option 2: Brava composite cedar shake, Class A fire rated, 50-year warranty
  • Option 3: new cedar, where heritage or neighbourhood requires it
  • 10-year Stonnex labour warranty on shingle, metal and composite installations
Three Paths

Cedar Replacement Options Compared

Architectural Shingle

$7.50 to $11.50 per sq ft installed including the new deck. 20 to 30 years. Class A fire rating. The right answer for most homes where the look is not the priority.

Brava Composite Shake

Indistinguishable from hand-split cedar at the curb, Class A fire rated, 50-year transferable warranty, no moss treatment ever. Priced per project; see our Brava page.

New Cedar Shake

The authentic material, 15 to 25 years on the wet coast with regular treatment. Class C fire rating unless treated; increasingly restricted by municipal bylaws and insurers.

Standing-Seam Metal

A fourth option on contemporary homes: 50-plus years, Class A, $13 to $19 per sq ft. Changes the look entirely, so it suits some houses and not others.

Why Cedar Conversions Cost More Than a Re-Roof

Cedar shakes are nailed to 1x4 or 1x6 skip sheathing with air gaps that let the wood dry. Shingles, composite shake and metal all need a continuous deck. A cedar conversion therefore includes removing the shakes and the skip sheathing (or sheathing over it where the structure allows), installing new plywood across the entire roof, then building the new roof on top. The plywood alone is 60 to 80 sheets on a typical home, which is why the installed range of $7.50 to $11.50 per square foot sits above a standard shingle replacement.

A recent example: a cedar-to-shingle conversion in Abbotsford involved full shake removal, a new 1/2-inch plywood deck, synthetic underlayment, ice and water membrane at all eaves and valleys, and architectural shingles, completed over four working days.

The Fire-Rating Question

Untreated cedar is a Class C roof covering. Several Lower Mainland municipalities now restrict new untreated wood roofs in wildland interface zones, and some insurers surcharge or decline homes with cedar roofs. Architectural shingles, Brava composite shake and metal are all Class A, the highest rating. If you are in an interface area on the North Shore, in Maple Ridge, Mission, Abbotsford or the Fraser Valley hillsides, this alone often decides the material. Our cedar shake fire rules guide covers the municipal detail.

Keeping the Cedar Look: Brava Composite Shake

For heritage homes in Shaughnessy and Kerrisdale, West Vancouver character properties and anywhere the cedar profile is part of the architecture, Brava composite shake is the material we recommend most often. It is moulded from real hand-split cedar, colour runs through the full thickness of the tile, it carries a Class A rating and a 50-year transferable warranty, and it never needs moss treatment or oiling. Stonnex is one of the few Brava Preferred Contractors in British Columbia. Details, colours and completed projects are on our Brava page; costs are in the Brava cost guide, and a side-by-side is in Brava versus real cedar.

Signs Your Cedar Roof Is Done

  • Shakes splitting along the grain and cupping at the edges across whole slopes
  • Thick moss that returns within a season of cleaning; the wood beneath is staying wet
  • Daylight visible through the roof from the attic
  • Repeated leaks at valleys and around chimneys despite repairs
  • Shakes worn thin enough to see the fastener heads

At that point, pressure-washing and treating is money spent on a roof with two or three years left. A free inspection gives you a written answer and a quote for whichever path suits the house. Full pricing method is in the roof replacement cost guide, or call 604-935-6976.

Common Questions

Cedar Replacement
FAQs

How much does it cost to replace a cedar shake roof in BC?
Converting cedar to architectural shingles runs $7.50 to $11.50 per square foot installed, or roughly $13,000 to $20,000 for a typical detached home, before GST. The range is higher than a standard re-roof because a new plywood deck is required over the skip sheathing. Brava composite shake and new cedar are priced per project.
Can you put shingles directly over cedar shakes?
No. Shakes sit on skip sheathing with gaps, and shingles need a continuous deck. The shakes come off, the deck is sheathed in plywood, and the new roof is built on that. Anyone proposing to shingle over cedar is proposing a roof that will fail.
Is cedar shake still allowed in Metro Vancouver?
Generally yes, but with growing restrictions. Several municipalities limit new untreated wood roofing in wildland interface zones, and some insurers decline or surcharge cedar roofs. Treated cedar, architectural shingle, Brava composite and metal are all Class A alternatives. Our fire rules guide covers the municipal specifics.
How long does a cedar shake roof last on the coast?
Fifteen to 25 years with regular cleaning and treatment, often less on shaded north-facing slopes where moss holds moisture. Most cedar roofs we replace are 25 to 35 years old and have been leaking for some time.
What is the closest match to real cedar without the maintenance?
Brava composite cedar shake. It is moulded from real split cedar, carries colour through the full tile, is Class A fire rated and warranted for 50 years, and needs no moss treatment or oiling. Stonnex is a Brava Preferred Contractor; see the Brava page for colours and completed Vancouver projects.
How long does a cedar conversion take?
Three to five working days for a typical detached home, longer for large or complex roofs. The roof is watertight at the end of every day; we never leave a stripped deck open overnight.
Does insurance pay for a cedar roof replacement?
Normal wear is not covered, but replacing an aging cedar roof with a Class A material often lowers your premium, and some insurers require it at renewal. Ask your broker before choosing a material; the answer sometimes changes the decision.
