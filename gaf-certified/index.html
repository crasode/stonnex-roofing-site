GAF Certified Roofing Contractor

Residential Roofing Contractor  ·  Lower Mainland, BC

Stonnex Roofing is a GAF Certified Residential Roofing Contractor. GAF is North America's largest roofing manufacturer, and the certification is granted by GAF, not by us. It is checkable: you can see our listing in GAF's contractor directory. It is also the reason we can register manufacturer warranties on the roofs we install.

1. What the certification actually means

Roofing has very few real credentials. British Columbia does not license roofing contractors, so a lot of what gets called a “certification” in this trade is not verifiable by anyone. GAF certification is different, because GAF grants it and GAF can revoke it.

To hold it, a contractor has to carry proper insurance, maintain a satisfactory reputation with GAF, and stay in good standing. It is a manufacturer's designation covering the company, and it is what allows us to register GAF's manufacturer warranties on your roof when GAF materials are specified.

2. What it means for your roof

  • We install GAF systems to the manufacturer's published specifications, which is what keeps their material warranty valid
  • We register your GAF materials with the manufacturer on completion and give you the documentation
  • You get access to GAF's warranty options on qualifying systems, described in the chart below

Worth being precise about the difference, because it is the thing most often blurred in this industry: GAF warranties cover materials and are provided by GAF. The workmanship warranty on our installation is separate, is provided by Stonnex Construction Ltd., and runs 10 years on shingle and metal and 5 years on torch-on and flat systems. Both are set out on our warranty page.

GAF Certification Questions

What the certification means, and how to check it.

What does GAF certification actually mean?

GAF is North America's largest roofing manufacturer, and the certification is granted by GAF rather than claimed by us. To hold it a contractor has to carry proper insurance, maintain a satisfactory reputation with GAF, and stay in good standing. GAF can revoke it, which is what makes it worth something.

Can I verify that Stonnex is really GAF certified?

Yes, and you should. We are listed in GAF's own contractor directory, linked from this page. Do not take a certification claim from any contractor's website at face value when the manufacturer publishes a directory you can check.

Is roofing licensed in British Columbia?

No. British Columbia does not license roofing contractors, which is why manufacturer certifications, WorkSafeBC registration and proof of liability insurance are the credentials worth checking. Anyone can call themselves a roofer here.

Does GAF certification mean GAF warrants my roof?

It means we can register manufacturer warranties on the GAF systems we install. The material warranty is between you and GAF. Our own 10-year labour warranty is separate and comes from us — see our warranty page for how the two differ.

Do you only install GAF products?

No. GAF is what we install most in asphalt shingle, but we also install metal systems and Brava composite shake as a Brava Preferred Contractor. We specify what suits the roof rather than what suits one supplier.
