Common Questions

Roofing in Coquitlam
Answered.

What does roof replacement cost in Coquitlam?
Coquitlam residential roof replacement typically ranges from $11,000 to $23,000 depending on home size, pitch, and current deck condition. Stonnex provides written fixed-price quotes after a complimentary on-site inspection — no guessing.
How do I know if my Coquitlam roof needs replacing?
Key indicators include granule loss visible in gutters, curling or missing shingles, dark staining from algae, sagging sections, or interior water staining. Homes built in Coquitlam between 1988 and 2005 are statistically entering the primary replacement window now.
Does Stonnex do emergency roof repairs in Coquitlam?
Yes. For active leaks or storm damage, we offer priority inspection scheduling. We can provide temporary weatherproofing while materials are ordered and a replacement date is set.
How long does a Coquitlam roof replacement take?
Most standard Coquitlam residential roof replacements are completed in one to two days. We confirm timeline in the written quote based on your home's specific roofline.
