Roof Replacement Cost in Maple Ridge in 2026: Prices by Size and Material

Published July 17, 2026 · Stonnex Roofing

If your Maple Ridge home is overdue for a new roof, the first thing you want to know is what it's going to cost. That number depends on your roof's size, the material you choose, and what's going on underneath the existing surface. This guide covers realistic 2026 price ranges for Maple Ridge specifically, explains what pushes costs up or down, and gives you a clearer picture of what you're actually paying for.

What Drives Roof Replacement Costs in Maple Ridge

Maple Ridge sits in the Fraser Valley foothills, where annual rainfall regularly exceeds 1,200mm and winter freeze-thaw cycles put real stress on roofing systems. That climate matters when you're comparing quotes, because a system priced for drier Interior conditions may not hold up the same way here.

The main cost variables are roof area, pitch, material choice, and the condition of your existing deck. Tear-off and disposal of old shingles adds to the total, as does any rotted decking that needs to be replaced before new material goes down.

Labour rates across the Lower Mainland run higher than the provincial average, and Maple Ridge is no exception. Once you factor in WorkSafeBC compliance, liability insurance, and permit requirements, it becomes clear why quotes from credentialed contractors look different from those that skip those steps.

2026 Price Ranges by Material

Material choice has the biggest single impact on your total cost. Here's how the main options compare for a 2,000 sq ft roof in Maple Ridge in 2026.

Architectural Asphalt Shingles

The most common choice in the Fraser Valley, and for good reason. Architectural asphalt from manufacturers like IKO, GAF, and Malarkey performs well in BC's wet climate, with warranties ranging from 30 to 50 years depending on the product line. Installed cost for a 2,000 sq ft roof typically falls between $15,000 and $22,500.

For most Maple Ridge homes built in the 1990s and 2000s, architectural asphalt is the practical default. It handles rainfall and freeze-thaw reliably, and the warranty depth from major manufacturers is genuinely strong when the work is done by a certified contractor.

Cedar Shake

Cedar shake is common on older Maple Ridge homes and remains a popular aesthetic choice. It performs well when properly installed and maintained, but material costs are significantly higher than asphalt. Installed cost for a 2,000 sq ft cedar shake roof typically runs between $28,000 and $42,000 in 2026.

Cedar requires more upkeep over its lifespan and is more vulnerable to moss and moisture retention in the Fraser Valley's wet climate. If you're replacing an existing cedar roof, confirm with your insurer whether a specific treatment or fire rating is required before you commit to a material.

Standing Seam Metal

Standing seam metal is growing in popularity across the Lower Mainland, particularly on modern homes and coach houses. It handles BC rainfall exceptionally well, needs minimal maintenance, and carries some of the longest warranties available. Installed cost for a 2,000 sq ft standing seam roof typically ranges from $32,000 to $50,000 in 2026.

The higher upfront cost reflects both the material and the skilled labour required for proper installation. Over a 40 to 50 year lifespan, the per-year cost often compares favourably to multiple asphalt replacements. If you're planning to stay in your Maple Ridge home long-term, metal is worth pricing seriously. You can read more about how these materials stack up for BC's coastal conditions in this guide to the best roofing materials for the BC coast.

Slate

Slate is the premium end of the market. It's heavy, requires reinforced framing, and demands experienced installers. For a 2,000 sq ft roof, installed cost typically starts at $55,000 and can exceed $80,000 depending on slate origin and roof complexity. It's rare in Maple Ridge but does appear on heritage-style and custom homes.

Flat and Torch-On Membrane

Many Maple Ridge homes have a flat or low-slope section — typically over an attached garage, addition, or coach house. These areas can't be covered by a standard shingle system. Torch-on membrane and modified bitumen are the standard solution here, and pricing typically runs $8 to $14 per sq ft installed, depending on the number of plies and the condition of the existing substrate.

What the Quote Should Include

A complete roof replacement quote in Maple Ridge should break out the following:

  • Tear-off and disposal of existing material
  • Inspection and replacement of any damaged decking (priced per sheet)
  • Ice and water shield at eaves and valleys
  • Synthetic or felt underlayment across the full deck
  • Drip edge and flashing at all penetrations and transitions
  • The roofing material itself, with the specific product name and warranty tier
  • Cleanup and haul-away

If a quote doesn't itemise these, ask for the breakdown before signing anything. Vague line items are where surprises tend to hide. If you're not sure whether your roof needs a full replacement or targeted repairs, the signs you need a new roof article covers the key indicators.

How Maple Ridge Costs Compare to the Rest of Metro Vancouver

Maple Ridge sits in the mid-range of the Metro Vancouver pricing spectrum. Labour costs are slightly lower than in Vancouver, North Vancouver, or West Vancouver, but higher than some Fraser Valley communities further east. Material costs are consistent across the region since they're driven by manufacturer pricing, not geography.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cost to replace a roof in Maple Ridge in 2026?

For a typical 2,000 sq ft home with architectural asphalt shingles, expect to pay between $15,000 and $22,500 installed. Cedar shake runs $28,000 to $42,000 for the same size, and standing seam metal typically falls between $32,000 and $50,000. Flat membrane sections are priced separately at roughly $8 to $14 per sq ft.

Does roof pitch affect the price in Maple Ridge?

Yes. A steeper pitch adds 15 to 25 percent to labour costs because it slows installation and requires additional safety equipment. Most standard Maple Ridge homes fall into a moderate pitch range, but if your roofline is steep or complex, ask your contractor to specify the pitch multiplier in the quote.

How long does a roof replacement take in Maple Ridge?

Most residential replacements on a standard detached home take one to three days. Larger homes, complex rooflines, or significant deck repairs can extend that timeline. Weather is a real factor in the Fraser Valley, so contractors typically build buffer time into scheduling.

Can I get a roof replacement quote without calling anyone?

Yes. Stonnex's instant quote tool returns a real price estimate in approximately 60 seconds by address entry, using current IKO, GAF, and Malarkey pricing data. No phone call or appointment required.

What warranties are available on a new roof in Maple Ridge?

Coverage depends on the material and product line. Architectural asphalt from IKO, GAF, and Malarkey carries warranties ranging from 30 to 50 years when installed by a certified contractor. Standing seam metal warranties are similarly long. Ask your contractor to specify the warranty tier in writing before work begins.

Does a roof replacement in Maple Ridge require a permit?

Requirements vary by scope. A full tear-off and replacement typically requires a building permit from the City of Maple Ridge. Your contractor should pull the permit on your behalf as part of the job. If a contractor suggests skipping the permit to save money, treat that as a red flag.

What is the difference between a flat roof and a torch-on membrane system?

A flat or low-slope roof can't shed water the way a pitched roof does, so it requires a waterproof membrane rather than shingles. Torch-on membrane uses heat-welded modified bitumen layers to create a watertight surface. It's the standard solution for flat sections on Maple Ridge homes, including garages, additions, and coach houses.
