Roof Replacement Cost in New Westminster in 2026: What to Expect

Published July 22, 2026 · Stonnex Roofing

If your New Westminster home is more than 15 years old and you've started noticing curling shingles, granule loss in the gutters, or water stains on the ceiling, you're probably past the point of wondering whether something's wrong. Now you want to know what fixing it will actually cost.

This article breaks down what drives roof replacement pricing in New Westminster specifically — the materials, the variables, and how to get a real number without sitting through a sales pitch.

Why New Westminster Roofs Face Particular Pressure

New Westminster sits in the heart of Greater Vancouver, where annual rainfall averages around 1,200mm. That's not just wet winters — it's sustained moisture exposure across every season, layered on top of the temperature swings that cause roofing materials to expand and contract year after year.

Older homes in Sapperton, Glenbrooke North, and the West End were frequently built with cedar shake roofs that are now well past their serviceable life. Many of the city's detached homes also sit on compact lots with steeper pitches, which affects both how much material a job requires and how long it takes to install. All of that feeds directly into your replacement cost.

What Determines Your Roof Replacement Cost

No two roofs in New Westminster cost exactly the same. The main variables are:

Roof size and pitch. Roofing is priced per square — that's 100 square feet. A steeper pitch requires more safety equipment and slower, more careful installation, which drives up labour. A typical New Westminster detached home runs somewhere between 18 and 30 squares depending on footprint and roof design.

Material choice. Architectural asphalt shingles sit at the lower end of the cost range. Cedar shake, standing seam metal, and slate each carry progressively higher material costs, and each performs differently in BC's coastal climate.

Existing roof condition. Multiple layers of old shingles mean more teardown and higher disposal costs. Damaged decking found during teardown adds to the final number.

Accessibility and complexity. Dormers, skylights, chimneys, and multiple valleys all add time. A clean gable roof costs less to replace than a complex hip roof with several penetrations.

Gutters and flashings. Flashings around chimneys and skylights often need replacing at the same time as the roof. If your gutters are original to the home, this is a practical moment to address them too.

Material Cost Ranges for New Westminster in 2026

The ranges below reflect full replacements — teardown, disposal, underlayment, flashings, and installation — based on what Stonnex installs across the Lower Mainland.

Architectural Asphalt Shingles

The most common choice for New Westminster homes. IKO, GAF, and Malarkey all produce asphalt shingles built for high-moisture climates, and their current warranty offerings run up to 50 years — a meaningful step up from the 25-year products that were standard a generation ago.

Typical range: $8,000 to $16,000 for a standard detached home, depending on size and complexity.

Cedar Shake

Cedar shake is common on older New Westminster homes and carries a premium in both materials and installation time. With the right underlayment and proper installation, it holds up well in coastal conditions — but it requires more maintenance than asphalt and isn't the right fit for every pitch or exposure.

Typical range: $18,000 to $30,000+, depending on shake grade and roof complexity.

Standing Seam Metal

Metal roofing has grown steadily in popularity across Greater Vancouver, and for good reason. Standing seam panels shed water efficiently, resist moss better than asphalt, and can last 40 to 50 years with minimal upkeep. The upfront cost is higher, but the long-term math often works in its favour.

Typical range: $22,000 to $40,000+ for a residential installation.

Slate

Genuine slate is the highest-cost option, typically reserved for heritage properties or custom builds. It also requires a structural assessment to confirm the framing can carry the weight before any work begins.

Typical range: $30,000 to $60,000+, depending on roof size and slate grade.

Choosing the Right Material for a New Westminster Home

The right material comes down to your budget, your home's age and structure, and how long you plan to stay. For most New Westminster homeowners replacing a 20-to-30-year-old asphalt roof, architectural shingles from IKO, GAF, or Malarkey offer the strongest combination of performance, warranty depth, and cost. The 50-year warranty products from these manufacturers are worth understanding before you decide — they represent a genuine difference in long-term protection, not just a marketing upgrade.

If you're replacing cedar shake and want to preserve the character of an older home, modern synthetic shake alternatives are worth considering. They perform better in sustained moisture than natural cedar while maintaining a similar look.

Signs It’s Time to Replace, Not Just Repair

Not every roof problem calls for a full replacement. But certain conditions signal that repairs are no longer cost-effective.

If your roof is showing multiple failing areas, has granule loss across more than a third of the surface, or has been patched two or more times in the past five years, replacement is likely the more economical path.
