Roofing Companies in Surrey BC: What to Look For and How Stonnex Compares

Published July 28, 2026 · Stonnex Roofing

Surrey is one of BC’s fastest-growing cities, and that growth keeps local roofers busy. New builds, aging homes, storm damage, and routine wear all generate steady demand. But a crowded market makes it harder to separate the contractors worth hiring from the ones you’ll regret calling.

This guide covers what actually matters when comparing roofing companies in Surrey BC, the questions worth asking before you sign anything, and how Stonnex approaches the work.

Why Surrey Homeowners Face Unique Roofing Challenges

Surrey sits at the wetter end of the Lower Mainland. Heavy rainfall runs from fall through spring, and many neighbourhoods have mature tree canopy that deposits debris on roofs year-round. That combination accelerates moss growth, clogs gutters, and puts real stress on flashing and valleys.

Homes across Surrey also span a wide range of ages and styles. A 1970s bungalow in Newton has very different needs than a newer townhome in Clayton Heights. The right contractor needs to work comfortably across asphalt shingles, flat membrane systems, and everything in between.

What to Look For When Comparing Roofing Companies in Surrey BC

Licensing and Insurance

This is the baseline. Any roofing contractor working in BC should hold a valid business licence and carry both liability insurance and WorkSafeBC coverage. If a company can’t confirm both, move on. Liability coverage protects your property if something goes wrong; WorkSafeBC coverage protects you from being held responsible if a worker is injured on your roof.

Ask for certificates of insurance directly — not just a verbal confirmation.

Local Experience and Reputation

A company that has been working in Surrey and the broader Fraser Valley understands local building codes, permit requirements, and the weather patterns that affect how materials perform here. Online reviews matter, but look for patterns rather than individual comments. Consistent mentions of punctuality, clean job sites, and clear communication tell you more than a handful of five-star ratings.

Transparency on Materials and Warranties

Not every contractor installs the same grade of shingle or membrane, and the difference matters. Ask specifically which manufacturer and product line they use, and what warranty comes with it. Manufacturer warranties and contractor workmanship warranties are separate things — both matter. A strong manufacturer warranty, like the 50-year coverage Stonnex backs its projects with, reflects both product quality and the installer’s certification status with that manufacturer.

For more on how different materials hold up in BC’s coastal climate, this breakdown of the best roofing materials for the BC coast is worth reading.

A Clear Process and a Named Point of Contact

Poor communication is one of the most common complaints homeowners have about roofing contractors. Projects start, then no one can be reached. Materials show up without warning. The crew changes between days. On a project that costs thousands of dollars and affects your home’s structural integrity, none of that is acceptable.

Ask any company you’re considering: who is my point of contact from start to finish? A vague answer is a signal.

Honest Quoting

A suspiciously low quote usually means something is missing — underlayment, proper disposal of old materials, flashing replacement, or ventilation work. A reputable company gives you a detailed, itemized quote so you know exactly what’s included. Get at least two or three quotes and compare them line by line, not just the bottom number.

If you want a sense of what roof replacement typically costs in this region, the roof replacement cost guide for Metro Vancouver breaks down the main cost drivers.

Red Flags to Watch For

Door-to-door solicitation after a storm. Legitimate contractors don’t chase storm damage. Companies that show up unsolicited right after heavy weather are often out-of-province operators looking for quick work with no long-term accountability.

Pressure to sign immediately. Any contractor pushing you to commit on the spot is more interested in closing than in doing right by you.

No written contract. Scope, materials, timeline, payment schedule, and warranty terms should all be in writing.

Payment in full upfront. A deposit is normal. Paying the entire job before work begins is not.

Don’t Wait Until It’s Urgent

Many Surrey homeowners put off roofing work until a leak becomes impossible to ignore. By that point, water has often been getting in for months, and what could have been a repair has turned into a full replacement. Knowing the signs that you need a new roof can save you real money and stress.

If your roof is more than 15 years old, has visible moss or granule loss, or you’ve noticed ceiling staining inside, an inspection is worth booking now rather than waiting for the problem to get worse.

Making the Final Decision

Comparing roofing companies in Surrey BC comes down to a few clear criteria: proper licensing and insurance, verifiable local experience, honest and detailed quoting, quality materials with real warranties, and a communication structure that keeps you informed throughout.

No single company is right for every homeowner, but the ones worth hiring share those qualities. Take your time, ask direct questions, and don’t let urgency push you into a decision you haven’t thought through.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I ask a roofing company before hiring them in Surrey BC?

Ask for proof of liability insurance and WorkSafeBC coverage, the specific materials and manufacturer they use, what warranties apply to both materials and workmanship, who your point of contact will be throughout the project, and a detailed written quote. These questions separate prepared, professional contractors from those cutting corners.

How much does a roof replacement cost in Surrey BC?

Costs vary based on roof size, pitch, material type, and the condition of the existing structure. Most residential replacements in the Metro Vancouver region fall within a broad range depending on those variables. A detailed breakdown is available in the roof replacement cost guide for Metro Vancouver.

How long does a roof replacement take in Surrey?

Most standard residential replacements take one to two days for the installation itself. Larger homes, complex roof geometry, or flat membrane systems may take longer. Weather delays can also affect scheduling, particularly in fall and winter.

Is a flat roof or a sloped roof better for a Surrey home?

It depends on the structure of your home. Flat and low-slope roofs are common on modern builds, additions, and some older ranchers — they require different materials and maintenance than sloped roofs. Stonnex installs both and can advise on the right approach for your specific situation.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

Age, the extent of damage, and the condition of the underlying structure are the main factors. A roof under 10 years old with isolated damage often warrants a repair. One that’s 20-plus years old with widespread wear, moss penetration, or failing shingles usually makes more sense to replace. A professional inspection gives you a clear answer without the guesswork.

What is a manufacturer warranty and why does it matter?

A manufacturer warranty covers defects in the roofing materials themselves, separate from the contractor’s workmanship warranty. Extended manufacturer warranties — such as the 50-year coverage available through Stonnex — typically require the installer to be certified by that manufacturer. That means the warranty reflects both product quality and installation standards, not just a number on a brochure.
