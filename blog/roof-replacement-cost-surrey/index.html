Roof Replacement Cost in Surrey in 2026: Prices by Material and Home Size

Published July 6, 2026 · Stonnex Roofing

Surrey homeowners replacing a roof in 2026 are looking at a wide price range. The honest answer to “how much will this cost?” starts around $9,000 for a modest rancher with architectural asphalt shingles and can reach $40,000 or more for a larger two-storey home in standing seam metal or cedar shake.

This guide breaks down what you can realistically expect to pay in Surrey — by material and by square footage — with specific numbers rather than vague ranges. It also explains why Surrey’s particular housing stock affects your final cost in ways a generic BC estimate won’t capture.

Why Surrey Roof Replacement Costs Differ from the Rest of Metro Vancouver

Surrey is the largest city in the Fraser Valley by population, and its housing stock reflects decades of rapid suburban growth. Compact 1960s and 1970s ranchers in Whalley and Newton sit alongside large two-storey homes from the 1990s and 2000s in South Surrey, Clayton, and Cloverdale.

That mix matters for pricing. A single-storey rancher with a simple gable roof costs less to reroof than a two-storey home with multiple valleys, dormers, or a steeper pitch. Labour rates in Surrey are comparable to the rest of the Lower Mainland, but the wide variation in home styles means the per-square-foot price can swing significantly even between homes of similar size.

Surrey also sits firmly in BC’s wet coastal climate, receiving well over 1,200mm of rainfall annually. That moisture load is hard on roofing materials — moss takes hold on north-facing slopes, and granule loss accelerates on shingles exposed to constant wet-dry cycles. If your roof is past the 15 to 20-year mark, it’s worth knowing what a replacement actually costs before a leak forces the decision.

Surrey Roof Replacement Cost by Material in 2026

These price ranges reflect installed costs for a typical Surrey detached home, including tear-off of one existing layer, new underlayment, and disposal. All prices are in Canadian dollars.

Architectural Asphalt Shingles

Installed cost: $9,000 to $18,000 for most Surrey homes.

Architectural shingles — also called laminate shingles — are the most common choice in Surrey and across the Lower Mainland. Products from IKO, GAF, and Malarkey dominate the market here. They perform well in coastal conditions, carry manufacturer warranties up to 50 years depending on the product line, and come at multiple price points.

The lower end of this range applies to a smaller rancher with a simple roof profile. The upper end reflects a larger two-storey home with a steeper pitch, multiple valleys, or significant decking replacement.

Best for: Homeowners who want a proven, cost-effective material with strong warranty coverage and good moss resistance when treated.

Cedar Shake

Installed cost: $18,000 to $35,000 for most Surrey homes.

Cedar shake has a long history on the West Coast and still suits certain home styles well — particularly older craftsman and heritage-influenced homes in areas like Cloverdale and parts of South Surrey. The material looks distinctive and performs reasonably well when properly maintained.

The trade-off is real. Cedar requires more maintenance than asphalt in our wet coastal climate. Moss and moisture management are not optional if you want a cedar roof to reach its expected lifespan. Labour costs are also higher because installation is more involved and time-consuming.

Best for: Homeowners replacing an existing cedar roof on a character home who are committed to a maintenance schedule.

Standing Seam Metal

Installed cost: $22,000 to $42,000 for most Surrey homes.

Standing seam metal is the longest-lasting option available. It sheds moss and water beautifully — a real advantage in Surrey’s climate — and requires minimal maintenance over its lifespan. The up-front cost is the trade-off, and it’s a meaningful one.

Metal suits the modern architectural styles increasingly common in newer Surrey developments, as well as homeowners who plan to stay long-term and want to avoid another replacement cycle.

Best for: Homeowners with a longer time horizon who want the lowest lifetime cost and the least ongoing maintenance.

Torch-On Flat Membrane (SBS Membrane)

Installed cost: $8,000 to $20,000 depending on surface area and complexity.

Torch-on flat roofing uses a styrene-butadiene-styrene (SBS) modified bitumen membrane applied with heat. It’s the right system for flat or low-slope roofs, which appear on coach houses, secondary suites, and certain modern home styles throughout Surrey. A properly installed torch-on system handles standing water and the sustained rainfall of BC’s coast effectively.

Best for: Coach houses, secondary suites, and any home with a flat or low-slope section that needs a reliable waterproof membrane system.

What Drives the Final Price Up (or Down)

Decking condition

Once tear-off is complete, the crew inspects the plywood or OSB decking underneath. In older Surrey homes — particularly those built before 1990 — it’s common to find rot or delamination around chimneys, valleys, and low-slope transitions. Replacing damaged decking adds cost, but it’s not optional. Laying new shingles over compromised decking shortens the life of the entire roof.

Roof pitch and complexity

Steeper roofs require additional safety equipment and take longer to work on. A layout with multiple hips, valleys, or dormers takes more labour and generates more material waste than a simple gable. Both factors push the price up.

Number of existing layers

BC building code generally allows two layers of asphalt shingles before a full tear-off is required. If your home already has two layers, tear-off costs more and disposal fees increase. Some older Surrey homes have had multiple reroofing jobs done over the decades — discovering this during inspection changes the project scope.

Gutters and flashings

Replacing flashings around chimneys, skylights, and walls is standard practice during a full replacement. If your gutters are also near the end of their life, combining that work with the roof replacement saves on mobilisation costs. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a roof replacement cost in Surrey, BC in 2026?

Most Surrey homeowners pay between $9,000 and $18,000 for architectural asphalt shingles, $18,000 to $35,000 for cedar shake, and $22,000 to $42,000 for standing seam metal. The final cost depends on your home’s size, roof pitch, decking condition, and the number of existing layers being removed.

What is the cheapest roofing material for a Surrey home?

Architectural asphalt shingles are the most affordable installed option. Products from IKO, GAF, and Malarkey start at roughly $9,000 for a smaller rancher and carry manufacturer warranties up to 50 years on select product lines.

How long does a roof replacement take in Surrey?

Most residential replacements take one to three days for asphalt shingles, depending on home size and roof complexity. Cedar shake and standing seam metal installations typically take longer due to more involved installation processes.

Yes. Steeper roofs require additional safety equipment and more labour hours. A home with a 6:12 pitch or higher will generally cost more per square to reroof than a low-slope or flat section.
