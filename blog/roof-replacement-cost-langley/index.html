What a New Roof Costs in Langley (2026)

Published 9 September 2026 · Stonnex Roofing

Roof quotes in Langley vary more than people expect — not because contractors are pricing arbitrarily, but because "a house in Langley" covers a compact place in Langley City and a five-bedroom Willoughby build and a farmhouse out past Aldergrove. Those are genuinely different jobs.

Here is what the numbers actually look like, and what moves them.

The range

For a full tear-off and replacement in architectural asphalt shingle, most Langley homes land somewhere between $10,500 and $26,000. That is a wide band, so it helps to see where different properties fall in it.

Property typeTypical rangeWhat drives it
City of Langley, compact home$10,500 – $15,000Smaller area, simple roofline, easy access
Walnut Grove / Brookswood / Murrayville$14,000 – $20,0001980s–2000s stock, mature trees, moss on shaded slopes
Willoughby / Yorkson newer build$16,000 – $24,000Larger footprint, many dormers and bump-outs, more flashing
Township acreage$20,000 – $26,000+Large roof, access constraints, often outbuildings too

Metal and composite shake sit above these figures. Our composite shake cost guide covers that separately, and the region-wide 2026 guide breaks the numbers down by material.

What actually moves the price

Area, not floor plan

The single biggest factor is roof area, and it is not the same as the square footage of the house. A steeper pitch means more roof over the same floor plan — two houses of identical size can differ by 40% in actual roof area. It is also why a phone quote based on "it's a 2,400 square foot house" is close to meaningless.

Complexity

Every valley, dormer, skylight and chimney is flashing work, and flashing is where roofs leak. The Willoughby and Yorkson builds are the clearest example in Langley: attractive rooflines with a lot of changes of plane, each one adding labour that square footage alone does not capture.

What is under the shingles

You cannot see the decking until the old roof is off. On most Langley homes it is sound. Where it is not — usually localised rot around a chimney or a long-standing valley leak — it has to be replaced. Any quote should tell you the per-sheet rate for that in advance so it is not a surprise. We show you the affected area and get your approval before doing the work.

Access

Out on the Township acreages this becomes real money. A long driveway, outbuildings in the way, and nowhere convenient to put a bin all add time. It belongs in the quote from the start.

Ventilation

A lot of the 1990s and 2000s stock in Walnut Grove and Brookswood was built with ventilation that was marginal then and is behind current practice now. Correcting it during a reroof adds comparatively little and materially extends the life of the new roof. If a quote does not mention ventilation at all, ask why.

How to compare quotes properly

The most common mistake is comparing the totals. Three quotes for a Langley roof can differ by $6,000 and all be reasonable, because they are not for the same work. Check that each one states:

  • Full tear-off to the deck, or going over the existing layer (these are not equivalent)
  • The specific shingle line, not just a brand
  • Underlayment and ice-and-water membrane at eaves and valleys
  • New flashings, or reusing the existing ones
  • Ventilation — what is being added or corrected
  • The rate for replacing rotten decking, agreed before it is found
  • The labour warranty, separately from the manufacturer material warranty

That last one matters more than most homeowners realise. A 50-year shingle warranty covers the shingle, not the work of installing it. The two are different documents from different parties — our warranty page sets out how they differ.

A note on the cheapest quote

British Columbia does not license roofing contractors. Anyone can call themselves a roofer here, which is why the spread between quotes can be so wide. Before comparing prices, ask all three for a WorkSafeBC clearance letter, proof of liability insurance, and a manufacturer certification you can look up in the manufacturer's own directory. If one of them cannot produce all three, the price is not really comparable.

Common questions

How much is a new roof in Langley?

Most Langley homes land between about $10,500 and $26,000 for a full asphalt shingle replacement. Compact City of Langley homes and smaller townhouse-style roofs sit at the lower end; larger Willoughby houses with complex rooflines and Township acreage properties sit at the upper end, and can exceed it.

Why is my Langley quote higher than my neighbour's?

Usually roof area and complexity rather than anything suspicious. Two houses on the same street can differ by 40% in actual roof area once pitch is accounted for, and every dormer, valley and skylight adds flashing work that does not show up in square footage.

Does an acreage property cost more to roof?

Generally yes, and not only because the roof is bigger. Long driveways, outbuildings in the way and limited room to place a bin all add time. It is worth having the quote reflect access rather than discovering it mid-job.

Should I repair or replace?

If the roof is under about 15 years old and the problem is localised, repair. If it is past 20, showing granule loss across the whole surface and you are patching more than once a year, you are paying twice. An inspection should tell you which, with photographs.

When is the best time to reroof in Langley?

Late spring through early autumn is easiest, but work happens year round here. What matters more is not waiting until an active leak forces the decision, because that is when you have the least leverage on price and timing.
