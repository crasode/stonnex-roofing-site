Roof Replacement Cost in North Vancouver in 2026: A Neighbourhood-by-Neighbourhood Guide

Published July 19, 2026 · Stonnex Roofing

If you own a home in North Vancouver and you're starting to think about a roof replacement, the first question is almost always the same: what's this going to cost? The honest answer is that it depends on where you live, what your roof looks like, and what materials you choose.

North Vancouver is not a uniform market. A rancher in Lynn Valley has a very different roof profile than a steep-pitch craftsman in Edgemont Village or a newer townhome in Lower Lonsdale. Pitch, size, access, material, and neighbourhood all move the final number.

This guide breaks down what North Vancouver homeowners are actually paying for roof replacements in 2026, neighbourhood by neighbourhood, and explains what drives those differences.

What Drives Roof Replacement Cost in North Vancouver

Before the neighbourhood breakdown, it helps to understand which variables matter most.

Roof Size and Pitch

Roofing is priced per square — that's 100 square feet. A typical North Vancouver detached home runs anywhere from 18 to 35 squares depending on footprint and pitch. Steeper roofs take longer to work on safely, and that time shows up in the labour cost.

Material Choice

This is often the single biggest variable. Architectural asphalt shingles from IKO, GAF, or Malarkey sit at the accessible end of the range. Cedar shake, standing seam metal, and slate all carry higher material and labour costs, but they also offer longer lifespans and, in premium neighbourhoods, stronger curb appeal.

Access and Site Conditions

North Vancouver's topography creates real access challenges. Homes on hillsides in Grouse Woods, Edgemont, or the British Properties often require more staging time and specialized equipment — and that adds to the labour line.

Tear-Off and Decking

If your existing roof has two or more layers of shingles, or if the decking underneath shows rot or damage, those are additional costs. A written inspection report will tell you exactly what's underneath before any work begins.

Gutters and Flashings

Most roof replacements include new flashings. Gutter replacement is often done at the same time, which tends to be a cost-efficient decision given how much rain the North Shore gets — well over 1,200mm annually. Aging gutters compound the damage a worn roof causes, so it's worth addressing both together.

North Vancouver Neighbourhood Cost Breakdown (2026)

The ranges below reflect typical full roof replacements using architectural asphalt shingles as the base material. Cedar shake and metal will push costs higher. These are estimates to help frame your expectations — your actual quote depends on a site-specific assessment.

Lower Lonsdale and Central Lonsdale

Homes here tend to be a mix of older character houses and newer infill builds on relatively flat or moderate-pitch lots. Roof sizes typically run 18 to 25 squares. For a standard detached home, expect architectural asphalt replacement costs in the range of $12,000 to $18,000.

Older homes in this area sometimes have original cedar shake that hasn't been touched in 30 or 40 years. Replacing shake with shake costs considerably more. Switching to architectural asphalt is straightforward, but it does require proper underlayment suited to coastal conditions.

Lynn Valley

Lynn Valley is one of North Vancouver's most active roofing markets. Homes here are predominantly ranchers and split-levels built between the 1960s and 1990s — many of which are now past the 25-year mark on their original or first-replacement roofs.

Roof sizes vary widely, from compact 20-square ranchers to larger two-storey homes at 30 squares or more. Architectural asphalt replacement typically runs $13,000 to $22,000 depending on size and condition. Cedar shake replacements on larger homes can reach $35,000 to $45,000 or more.

Lynn Valley homeowners often replace roofs ahead of a sale or after an insurance renewal flags the age of the existing material. If that's your situation, a written inspection report with photographs can be submitted directly to your insurer or lender.

Edgemont Village and Grouse Woods

This is one of the higher-cost areas for roofing in North Vancouver. Homes tend to be larger, with steeper pitches and more complex roof geometries. Many were built in the 1980s and 1990s with cedar shake, which is now at or past the end of its useful life.

Hillside lots can make access more involved, and some properties require additional staging. Architectural asphalt replacement on a typical Edgemont home runs $18,000 to $28,000. Cedar shake replacement on a larger home with a steep pitch can reach $50,000 to $65,000 depending on scope.

Standing seam metal is an increasingly popular choice in this neighbourhood. It handles heavy coastal rainfall without issue, performs well long-term, and the higher upfront cost becomes easier to justify when you factor in the lifespan.

British Properties (West Vancouver border area)

Homes in the British Properties are among the largest and most complex on the North Shore. Roof replacements here are major projects. A full architectural asphalt replacement on a large home can run $25,000 to $40,000 or more. Metal and slate roofing on estate-scale homes can reach significantly higher.

These properties often benefit from optional drone imagery during the inspection phase, which allows a thorough assessment of hard-to-access sections without additional risk.

Deep Cove and Dollarton

Homes in Deep Cove and Dollarton sit close to the water, which means salt air and moisture are constant factors. Roofing materials here need to be rated for coastal exposure — Malarkey's Vista AR and GAF's Timberline HDZ are both well-suited to this environment.

Home sizes range from modest cottages to larger custom builds. Typical replacement costs run $14,000 to $24,000 for architectural asphalt. The coastal context also makes regular gutter maintenance more important here than in drier inland areas.

Seymour and Indian Arm Corridor

This area includes newer developments and some older recreational properties. Roof profiles tend to be more straightforward, which keeps labour costs relatively contained. Architectural asphalt replacement typically runs $12,000 to $20,000 for a standard detached home.

Cedar Shake vs. Architectural Asphalt in North Vancouver

Many North Vancouver homes were originally built with cedar shake. It's a traditional West Coast material that looks exceptional when maintained, but it requires more upkeep and has a shorter effective lifespan in coastal conditions than most homeowners expect.

When cedar shake reaches the end of its life, you have two realistic paths: replace like for like, or switch to architectural asphalt or metal. The right roofing material for BC's coast comes down to your priorities around budget, maintenance, and longevity.

Architectural asphalt from IKO, GAF, or Malarkey now carries manufacturer warranties up to 50 years and is engineered specifically for high-rainfall environments. Standing seam metal offers even longer performance with minimal maintenance. Cedar shake remains a strong choice for homeowners who value the aesthetic and are prepared for the maintenance commitment that comes with it.

What North Vancouver Homeowners Often Overlook

Permit Requirements

Both the District of North Vancouver and the City of North Vancouver require building permits for full roof replacements in most cases. A reputable contractor handles this as part of the project, but it's worth confirming before work starts.

Skylights and Ventilation

If your home has existing skylights, a roof replacement is the right time to assess their condition. Stonnex is a VELUX-certified installer, so skylight replacement or new installation can be coordinated as part of the same project.

Roof ventilation is another item that often gets missed. Inadequate ventilation shortens shingle life and can void manufacturer warranties. A proper inspection will flag this before it becomes a problem.

Gutters

Given North Vancouver's rainfall, gutters are a functional necessity, not an aesthetic choice. Seamless aluminum gutters engineered for BC rainfall volumes are worth considering alongside a roof replacement — doing both at once is almost always less expensive than scheduling them separately.

FAQs

How much does a roof replacement cost in North Vancouver in 2026?

For a typical detached home using architectural asphalt shingles, costs generally range from $12,000 to $28,000 depending on the neighbourhood, roof size, pitch, and condition of the existing decking. Cedar shake and standing seam metal replacements run higher — often $35,000 to $65,000 or more on larger or steeper-pitch homes.

Which North Vancouver neighbourhoods have the highest roof replacement costs?

Edgemont Village, Grouse Woods, and the British Properties tend to have the highest costs due to larger homes, steeper pitches, more complex roof geometries, and hillside access requirements. Deep Cove and Dollarton also carry a coastal premium because materials need to be rated for salt air and high moisture exposure.

Does North Vancouver require a permit for a roof replacement?

Yes, in most cases. Both the District of North Vancouver and the City of North Vancouver require building permits for full roof replacements. A licensed contractor will manage the permit process as part of the project.

Is cedar shake worth replacing with cedar shake in North Vancouver?

It depends on your budget and how much maintenance you're willing to take on. Cedar shake looks exceptional and suits the West Coast aesthetic, but it requires more upkeep in coastal conditions and has a shorter effective lifespan than modern architectural asphalt or metal. Many North Vancouver homeowners switching from cedar choose architectural asphalt or standing seam metal for the lower maintenance and longer warranty coverage.

How do I get an accurate quote for my North Vancouver home without a sales call?

Enter your address into the instant quote tool at Stonnex.com and receive real pricing from IKO, GAF, and Malarkey in approximately 60 seconds — no phone call required. For a more detailed assessment, the free in-person inspection produces a written report with photographs at no cost and no obligation.

What warranties are available on roof replacements in North Vancouver?

Manufacturer warranties from IKO, GAF, and Malarkey extend up to 50 years depending on the product and installation tier. Workmanship warranties are separate and provided by the contractor. Confirm both before signing any contract.

How long does a roof replacement take in North Vancouver?

Most residential replacements take one to three days depending on the size, pitch, and complexity of the roof. Larger homes in Edgemont or the British Properties with more complex geometry may take longer. Your project manager will give you a specific timeline before work begins.
