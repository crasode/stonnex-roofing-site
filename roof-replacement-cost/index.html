How the Number Is Actually Built

Roofing is priced by the "square" (100 square feet of roof surface), not by the footprint of the house. Three steps get you from a floor plan to a roof area you can check a quote against.

  1. Footprint. The ground area the roof covers, including overhangs. A 40 by 30 foot house with 2-foot eaves is roughly 44 by 34, or about 1,500 square feet.
  2. Pitch factor. Slope adds surface. Multiply the footprint by 1.054 for a 4/12 roof, 1.083 for 5/12, 1.118 for 6/12, 1.158 for 7/12, 1.202 for 8/12, and 1.302 for 10/12.
  3. Waste. Add 10 percent for a simple gable, 12 to 15 percent for hips, valleys and dormers. Divide by 100 for squares.

Stonnex measures from aerial imagery before the inspection and confirms on the roof. If a quote you receive does not state the roof area in squares, ask for it; the per-square rate is the only honest way to compare bids.

Six Things That Move the Price

1. Pitch and height

Anything over 7/12 needs fall-arrest anchors, roof jacks and slower work. Two-storey and three-storey homes add staging time. Expect 15 to 30 percent more than the same roof on a walkable rancher.

2. Deck condition

Plywood is only replaced where the inspection or the tear-off finds rot or delamination. Stonnex quotes plywood as a separate line at a stated per-sheet price (currently $95 to $125 per sheet installed depending on thickness) so you never pay for sheets that were not needed. Skip-sheathed cedar roofs always need a full new deck; that is included in the cedar conversion range above.

3. Layers to remove

BC Building Code allows at most two layers of asphalt shingle. Removing two layers instead of one adds disposal weight and labour, typically $0.50 to $1.00 per square foot.

4. Penetrations and flashing

Every chimney, skylight, plumbing stack and wall intersection is a leak point that gets new 26-gauge metal, step flashing per course and peel-and-stick membrane underneath. A roof with two chimneys and three skylights costs more than a clean gable with one vent, and it should.

5. Material tier

Within asphalt, the jump from a standard architectural shingle to an impact-rated or designer profile is $0.50 to $5.50 per square foot. Metal and composite shake are different systems with different labour, not just different materials.

6. Access and disposal

Tight lanes, no driveway for a bin, or a long carry to the truck add hours. Disposal is charged at cost; a typical single-layer tear-off on a detached home fills one 20-yard bin.

Repair or Replace? The Honest Test

If the roof is under 15 years old and the problem is at one flashing, one valley, or a handful of shingles, repair it. Stonnex repairs start in the low hundreds and most are under $1,500.

Replace when repairs would exceed about a third of a new roof, when granule loss is widespread, when shingles are curling across whole slopes, or when the roof is past 20 years on the wet coast. A free inspection with photos gives you the answer in writing, and we will tell you if the right answer is "not yet."

Common Questions

Roof Cost
FAQs

How much does a new roof cost in Vancouver in 2026?
For a typical detached home with 1,800 to 2,200 square feet of roof surface, an architectural asphalt shingle replacement runs $9,000 to $17,000 before GST. Steep character homes, premium shingles and complex roofs run higher; the worked examples on this page show how the number is built.
What is the cost per square foot for roofing in BC?
Installed architectural asphalt is $5.00 to $9.00 per square foot of roof surface in 2026. Two-ply torch-on is $7.00 to $12.00, cedar-to-shingle conversion $7.50 to $11.50, and standing-seam metal $13.00 to $19.00, all before GST.
Is GST charged on roofing in BC?
Yes. Roofing labour and materials are subject to 5% GST. PST is included in the material cost by the supplier and is not charged separately to homeowners on an installed roofing contract. All figures on this page are before GST.
Why are quotes for the same roof so different?
Usually because they are not quoting the same roof. Check for the roof area in squares, the number of layers being removed, whether underlayment is synthetic or felt, whether valleys and step flashings are new metal or reused, and whether plywood is priced per sheet or hidden. Stonnex quotes list every one of these.
How much does it cost to replace a cedar shake roof?
Converting cedar shakes to architectural shingles runs $7.50 to $11.50 per square foot, or roughly $13,000 to $20,000 for a typical home, because the skip sheathing must be covered with a new plywood deck. Replacing cedar with Brava composite shake is priced separately; see the Brava cost guide.
How much is a flat roof replacement?
A two-ply SBS torch-on roof on a residential flat or low-slope section runs $7.00 to $12.00 per square foot, typically $7,000 to $14,000 for a detached home's flat area. Full commercial tear-offs with new drains and insulation are quoted individually; recent commercial projects have ranged to $27,500 and beyond.
Does the price include removing the old roof?
Yes. Every range on this page includes tear-off and disposal of one existing layer. A second layer adds about $0.50 to $1.00 per square foot.
How much does plywood replacement add?
Stonnex quotes plywood per sheet installed, currently $95 to $125 depending on thickness, and only charges for sheets actually replaced. A typical home needing spot repairs uses 4 to 10 sheets; a full re-deck of a smaller home was recently $3,500.
What warranty comes with a new roof?
A written Stonnex labour warranty of 10 years on shingle and metal roofs and 5 years on torch-on and flat roofs, plus the manufacturer's material warranty registered in your name, which runs up to 50 years depending on the product.
How long does a roof replacement take?
Most detached homes are completed in one to three days. Cedar conversions and metal roofs take three to five. The roof is always watertight before the crew leaves for the night.
Can I finance a new roof?
Stonnex does not offer in-house financing. Many homeowners use a home equity line of credit; CMHC-insured and major-bank renovation financing also apply to roofing. Ask about deposit and progress payment terms when you book.
Do you charge for the inspection or the quote?
No. The inspection, the measurement and the written itemized quote are free, with no obligation. Quotes hold for 30 days.
Is a metal roof worth the extra cost?
At $13.00 to $19.00 per square foot, standing-seam metal costs roughly double a shingle roof and is expected to last 50 years or more, versus 20 to 30 for architectural shingles on the wet coast. It makes the most sense on homes you plan to hold long term, on steep or high roofs where a second replacement would be costly, and where the look suits the architecture.
Do you price match?
We will walk through any competing quote with you line by line and explain every difference. If a quote is cheaper because a line is missing, we will show you which one. We do not remove scope to hit a number.
